First release of Field Emitters for Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge.

Field Emitter posts link up to 20 blocks apart into a five-block-high perimeter that follows the terrain. Field Rails mount on any block face to seal doorways, pits and shafts. Fields block hostile mobs, passive mobs, players, dropped items or other entities with optional age, type, tag, item, UUID and direction rules, and report crossings or presence over redstone. The Field Tuner opens controls, manages every loaded field remotely, samples mobs for filters and cycles colors.

Every emitter and rail accepts Forge Energy. Defaults are 2 FE per field block each tick, 100,000 FE storage and 10,000 FE/t transfer, adjustable in the server config. Survival recipes, a creative tab and drops for every emitter section are included.
