## Published description

Posts link. The field follows.

Field Emitters adds emitter posts that link up to 20 blocks apart and raise a five-block field that climbs the terrain between them. Seal doorways and shafts with rails, choose what gets through with blocking filters, read crossings as redstone pulses, and reach every loaded field from the handheld tuner.

Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge
