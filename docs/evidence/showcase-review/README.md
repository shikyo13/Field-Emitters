## Assessment

Field Emitters matches the shared design language but does not yet match the effectiveness of Flux Pylons. Its strongest subject—the moving forcefield—is not demonstrated in motion. Keep the current video as the published showcase for now, but the next cut should prioritize actual gameplay over additional title cards.

- **Good:** consistent condensed typography, restrained cyan/violet accents, clear alignment, generous margins, and clean studio hardware renders. No obvious clipping in the inspected output frames. Main headlines remain readable at reduced size.
- **Audio gap:** Field Emitters is completely silent. Flux Pylons includes a pylon hum and activation/deactivation accents. Not having sound assets in the mod is not a reason to omit an original editorial sound bed. Listening quality of the Flux mix was not assessed here.
- **0–9 seconds:** introduction and tuner hardware take almost a third of the film before showing a field in the world. Flux has a similar hardware-first structure, but Field Emitters would benefit more from an immediate impact or terrain-projection opening.
- **Approximately 9–14 seconds:** the terrain-following section uses day/night still captures with a slight digital zoom. The night image is stronger, but it does not show the field actually extending along the hill.
- **Approximately 14–18 seconds:** the doorway field is faint against the bright test floor. A floating support block, redstone blocks and neighboring fixtures compete with the subject. The new connected-impact footage is a much stronger demonstration than this static frame.
- **Approximately 18–27 seconds:** blocking and detection are consecutive GUI stills. Neither shows a mob being stopped, a permitted entity passing, or a lamp/counter responding. Flux's overview at least visibly substantiates its claim with a populated output graph and measured energy values. Both films would benefit from actual live interaction.
- **Pacing and reading:** Field Emitters packs seven scenes into 30 seconds versus six for Flux. Secondary labels and dense GUI details are small at phone size, and crossfades briefly superimpose different text/menus. Use one highlighted setting and one visible outcome per shot.
- **Stale compatibility footer:** the Field Emitters export says 1.0.0 / Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge throughout. This was accurate for its original upload but no longer describes the five-build 1.0.1 release. The listings now state the current compatibility explicitly.

## Recommended next cut

Open with an impact wave across stacked rails. Follow with terrain projection, hostile-versus-allowed crossing, a real detection pulse lighting a lamp, one brief remote-manager interaction, and a short hardware/end-card finish. Keep the shared visual style, add restrained original sound design, and update the compatibility footer. The existing published video was not replaced as part of this review.
