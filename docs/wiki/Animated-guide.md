# In-game guide

Open **Guide** in the Field Manager or emitter controls. Emitter controls open the chapter for the selected tab.

The guide covers building fields, bridges and domes, filters, redstone detection, damage, access cards, inventory checkpoints, network management, appearance, equipment and automation.

- Select a chapter, or use **Next lesson** and **Previous lesson**.
- **Pause** or Space pauses playback. **Replay lesson** or R restarts it.
- Click or drag the timeline to choose a point. With the timeline focused, Left/Right moves five seconds; Home/End jumps to the beginning or end.
- **Done** or Escape returns to the previous screen.
