# Rails

Field Rails are one-block strips that mount on any block face. Two rails facing each other up to 20 blocks apart span a one-block-wide field between them. Rails placed side by side share power and join into a continuous wall, floor or ceiling without seams.

## Placement

1. Place a rail on a wall, floor or ceiling. It faces away from the block it is mounted on.
2. Place a second rail on the opposite surface, facing the first one, up to 20 blocks away.
3. Add rails beside each pair to widen the field. Adjacent rails connect automatically.
4. Feed energy into any rail in the chain.

Rails on a floor and a ceiling make a vertical span. Choose **Field shape: Horizontal floor / ceiling** on the Connections tab when a horizontal span should lie flat instead of standing as a wall. The menu only offers shapes that fit the span.

## Chains

A chain of connected rails behaves as one field. The control screen edits the whole chain from any rail in it, so a doorway made of three strips keeps one set of filters, one counter and one output, the same way a perimeter of posts does.

Do not connect a perimeter post directly to a rail. Posts and rails use different projection geometry and link only to their own kind.

## Examples

- **Doorway:** three rails on the floor and three on the lintel above a three-wide door. Block hostile mobs, let players and passive mobs through.
- **Mob farm exit:** rails across a drop with *Hostile* checked and only the inward direction enabled, so mobs fall in but cannot climb out.
- **Item sorter gate:** rails across a water stream with *Drops* checked and *Count each item* on, sending one pulse per item to a counter.
